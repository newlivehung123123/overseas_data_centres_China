Overseas data centres of Chinese firms

Data-centre capacity outside mainland China in which Chinese firms hold a role, as owner, operator, tenant, user, builder, equipment supplier or financier. Every row cites the page that supports it, and a script has checked that the cited passage is on that page.

Red points mark places with at least one site, and blue points mark places with cloud regions only. Each point marks the centre of the city that the source names, not the building.

Rules recorded

US export rules apply to US-origin chips wherever the chips are shipped. Host-country rules apply in their own country. Each row gives the part of the rule that it records and the source.
