Share of the facilities listed in the market

A facility counts under the fund if CAF II finances the facility; otherwise under headquarters or control if any firm at the facility has its headquarters or controlling owner in mainland China; and otherwise under former subsidiaries, which are DayOne, Bridge Data Centres, Bitdeer and Megaspeed. In Johor, 20 of the 30 facilities with a Chinese role count only because the operator or owner was once part of a Chinese firm; without that ground, 10 of the 84 facilities (11.9 per cent) have a Chinese role. The number inside each coloured part of a bar is a count of facilities, and the scale along the bottom (the x-axis) gives those facilities as a share of all the facilities that Baxtel lists in the market. The bars exclude nine facilities where a news report or Baxtel’s description of the facility suggests that a Chinese firm operates, owns, rents or finances the facility, but later reporting doubts the report, Baxtel’s description is undated, the owner is not named, or the firms have not confirmed their involvement. Source: Overseas Data Centres of Chinese Firms, a dataset compiled by Jason Hung, November 2026, from Baxtel’s market lists and the sources cited for each facility.
