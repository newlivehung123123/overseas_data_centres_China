Each circle marks the town or city that the source gives for one or more sites; the area of the circle grows with the number of sites, and exact addresses are not plotted. A site counts under the fund if the China-ASEAN Investment Cooperation Fund II finances the site; otherwise under headquarters or control if any firm at the site has its headquarters or controlling owner in mainland China; and otherwise under former subsidiaries.
